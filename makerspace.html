TOOLSArduino / Servo Motors / 18650 Li-ion Cells / Buck Converter
STATUSIN DEVELOPMENT

Built a functional Iron Man Mark III-style helmet with an articulating faceplate that opens and closes like in the movie, driven by multiple servo motors coordinated through an Arduino. Getting the timing and linkage geometry right so the plates move cleanly without binding has been most of the mechanical work.

To get it running wireless instead of tethered to a bench supply, I built a custom rechargeable battery pack from reclaimed 18650 lithium-ion cells pulled from old laptop batteries, paired with a buck converter to regulate voltage and a charging circuit — so the helmet is self-powered and rechargeable like any other battery-powered device, not something that needs to stay plugged into a power supply.
